Read your own money
like a 10-K, not a lecture.

Every finance app can show you a number. None of them taught you what the number means on a real company first — then let you watch it show up on your own bank account. That's the gap. This page is the whole competitive picture at a glance, plus three ad concepts to test it.

04

What only we do

Teach it on a real company, then unlock it on you

Learn Costco's current ratio from its actual 10-K. The lesson instantly unlocks the same ratio, computed from your own numbers. No competitor found pairs company education with a personal metric.23

All five report pieces, one artifact, every month

Income statement + balance sheet + cash-flow summary + a 6-category letter-grade scorecard + company-benchmark captions. Every competitor we found ships at most two of these.24

A Wrapped-style share card that hides the numbers

Monzo and Spotify's recaps share the actual figures. Ours shares ratios and grades only — never a balance, never a return — by design, not by accident.25

05

Three ad concepts

— education, not advice. No shame. No return promises.
1
Open on a phone screen: a company's real 10-K, current ratio highlighted. "This is how Costco checks if it can pay its bills." Hook: real filing, not a stock tip.
2
Cut to the same screen shape, but it's your numbers. "Same math. Your accounts." The reveal: the lesson becomes personal.
3
"You're not bad with money. You just never got a report card for it." Shame-free reframe, not a judgment.
4
Quick flash of the letter-grade scorecard — neutral tone, an arrow pointing to one thing to learn next, no red panic color. Shows the artifact, not a balance.
5
End card: the redacted share card ("Liquidity: B+ · Learn what that means") with the line "Never shows your balance. Ever." CTA: "Get your Personal 10-K — free."
