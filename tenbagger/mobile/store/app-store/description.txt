Learn to read any public company's numbers, 3 minutes a day.

Tenbagger turns real company financials into short, game-like lessons. Every question is built from a company's own SEC filings, so you practise on the numbers analysts actually read: revenue, margins, cash flow, debt and valuation. No jargon walls, no stock tips.

HOW IT WORKS
• Bite-size lessons: multiple choice, true/false, number entry, compare and rank questions, each with an instant explanation.
• Every answer shows its source, for example "COST FY2025 10-K · gross profit / revenue", so you learn where numbers come from.
• A learning path from the basics (what a margin is) to cash flow, returns on capital and valuation.
• Streaks, XP and a daily goal keep the habit going. They come only from learning, never from trading.

PRACTISE ON REAL COMPANIES
• Beginner-friendly screener: start from preset screens or build your own with plain-English metric explanations.
• Company pages with key metrics and up to 10 years of history as simple charts.
• "Practice with this company" builds a drill from that company's own numbers.
